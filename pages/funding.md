## Funding

### Ongoing Grant Support

#### NIH R01AG084637 (MPI: Kim, Jiang)
- **Role:** PI  
- **Duration:** 07/01/2024 – 06/30/2029  
- **Title:** Data-driven Subtypes of Alzheimer's disease progression for targeted treatment  
- **Project Description:**  
  The goal of this project is to identify subgroups of patients showing similar progression patterns across multimodal data, including cognitive function, brain atrophy, and biomarkers, by harmonizing multiple completed clinical trial data.

---

#### NIH R01AG082721 (MPI: Kim, Jiang)
- **Role:** PI  
- **Duration:** 09/01/2023 – 05/31/2029  
- **Title:** Harmonizing multiple clinical trials for Alzheimer's disease to investigate differential responses to treatment via federated counterfactual learning  
- **Project Description:**  
  Drug development for treating Alzheimer's disease has been very challenging due to the differential responses of patients to different treatments. This project will develop machine learning models to identify patient subgroups who respond differently to treatments. This will result in smaller, less expensive studies, and more targeted Alzheimer's disease clinical trials that expose fewer patients to experimental medications to which they are unlikely to respond.

---

#### NIH U24AG098157 (Zhang, Liu, Cui)
- **Role:** Co-I  
- **Duration:** 09/2025 – 08/2030  
- **Title:** ReCARDO: Using Real-World Data to Derive Common Data Elements for Alzheimer's Disease and AD-Related Dementias  
- **Project Description:**  
  The goal of this project is to establish a ten-institution research network and an AD/ADRD-focused collaborative data ecosystem incorporating common data elements and real-world data. The network will support NIA-funded studies and enhance the transparency, rigor, and reproducibility of AD/ADRD research.

---

#### NIH R01HD120995-01 (Moreira)
- **Role:** Co-I  
- **Duration:** 07/2026 – 06/2030  
- **Title:** Defining, Validating, & Endotyping Phenotypes in BPD  
- **Project Description:**  
  The goal of this project is to define and validate clinically and biologically meaningful subgroups of bronchopulmonary dysplasia in preterm infants. Machine learning will be applied to large-scale neonatal datasets to identify clinical phenotypes, validate them across cohorts, and integrate transcriptomic data to identify molecular endotypes and pathways underlying disease progression.

---

#### NIH R01NR22258 (Mabry, Liu)
- **Role:** Site PI  
- **Duration:** 04/2026 – 03/2030  
- **Title:** An Artificial Intelligence Approach to Understanding Trade-offs in Emergency Department Decision-Making  
- **Project Description:**  
  This project addresses challenges faced by triage nurses and other emergency department (ED) team members when managing care in overcrowded ED settings. The project will develop an evaluation framework for ED decision-making and use it to assess and compare artificial intelligence models, including AI-Triage+, designed to support rapid and accurate ED decision-making and improve patient care.
